# P3 vocabulary sources and review limits

- Word heads, stable IDs, book categories and page references retain the user's existing extracted audit ledger for 《2025考研英语词汇闪过》. The original PDF and full 5,552-row ledger remain private; this pack does not reproduce its definitions, example sentences or audio.
- The 400 new Chinese common senses, English examples and translations were independently written and reviewed by AI. They are learning material, not official PETS definitions or personal confirmation. Existing content retains its original review status.
- PETS-3 2015 matching uses the appendix in the [Education Examinations Authority's public PDF](https://pets.neea.edu.cn/res/Home/2507/7186495d90963d37a36afccbfb505cc9.pdf). The preface is dated March 2015. Matching is case-sensitive against explicitly printed forms; absence is labelled 未直接列出, not official exclusion. All 2025 membership remains 待核实. Book frequency categories are not PETS frequency.
- WordNet 3.1 is used to check available lexical identities/parts of speech, not to copy Chinese definitions. Source: [Princeton WordNet](https://wordnetcode.princeton.edu/wn3.1.dict.tar.gz). The bundled `WORDNET_3_1_LICENSE.txt` applies.
- Manually specified spelling chunks and primary stress were cross-checked against [CMUdict](https://github.com/cmusphinx/cmudict). American pronunciation variants are possible. `CMUDICT_LICENSE.txt` is bundled. Phoneme notation is not displayed in the app; spelling chunks and uppercase stress are memory aids.
- The six existing recordings retain the authors, URLs and permissions in `assets/pronunciation/ATTRIBUTION.md`. The other 494 words have no bundled recording and are explicitly pending. No commercial dictionary audio was downloaded or fabricated.

Source and generated content hashes are recorded in `docs/p3/selection-statistics.json`.
